About

Trails, drones & the people who hike them.

I'm Kay Du — a Hiking Drone Creator based in Malaysia 🇲🇾. I'm running a personal project called 60 Mountains in Malaysia, documenting each summit and publishing free GPX navigation files so fellow hikers can plan with confidence.

I'm also Co-Founder of So Hikers Malaysia, alongside founder Khor Phor Xiang. Together we've grown one of Malaysia's most engaged outdoor communities — 300+ attendees across our events.

Beyond hiking, I work across content creation, drone piloting, video editing, and social media marketing — turning real trail experience into brand stories that don't feel manufactured.
