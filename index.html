About

Hi, I'm Kay Du.

Hi, I'm Kay Du, a Hiking Drone Creator based in Malaysia 🇲🇾

I'm currently running a personal project, "60 Mountains in Malaysia", where I hike and document 60 different mountains while sharing free GPX navigation files to help fellow hikers plan their routes with confidence.

I am the Co-Founder of So Hikers Malaysia, working alongside Khor Phor Xiang, the Founder of So Hikers Malaysia. Together, we build and grow the community through hike planning, content creation, workshops, and outdoor collaborations.

Beyond hiking, I have hands-on experience in content creation, drone piloting, video editing, and social media marketing. I enjoy turning real experiences into meaningful visuals — trail documentation, event highlights, or brand storytelling.
